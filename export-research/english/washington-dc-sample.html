The sample

Company-level rows with public evidence.

Address, phone, and coverage details are retained because a procurement or market-entry team needs reviewable locations. Personal contact information is excluded.

  1. 1

    Official location source

    Company website or official branch / office locator.

  2. 2

    Additional public source

    Government record, public-sector vendor file, association listing, regulatory profile, BBB, or another independent directory.

  3. 3

    Reviewed fields

    Company, service line, street address, city/state/ZIP, public company phone, and verification note.

  4. 4

    Explicit limits

    Drive time was not calculated for every row, and employee counts remain unknown unless supported.

Important limits

What this sample does and does not prove.

It demonstrates

  • A repeatable company and location schema
  • Source links and cross-check status
  • Duplicate and required-field validation
  • A company-only data boundary

It does not prove

  • That every address was visited or telephoned
  • Current employee or sales-team headcount
  • That all listed companies fit a future client brief
  • Drive-time compliance for every individual location

This is an independently prepared method sample, not a testimonial, customer case, or paid milestone. No individual names, personal emails, or personal phone numbers are included.

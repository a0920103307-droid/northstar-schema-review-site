Research method

Define relevance before collecting rows.

The research starts with the product, market, channel role, and exclusions. That definition determines which company facts matter and prevents volume from hiding poor-fit records.

  1. 1

    Define the scope

    Product line, market, customer type, channel role, existing network, and exclusions.

  2. 2

    Build the candidate pool

    Official websites, authorized-distributor locators, industry associations, trade directories, and public company records.

  3. 3

    Verify and score

    Check the role, location, products, principals, and service evidence, then apply the same fit rubric.

  4. 4

    Deliver the next questions

    Separate confirmed facts, reasonable inference, unverified assumptions, and open data gaps.

Boundaries

Useful evidence, without overstating certainty.

Included

  • Company-level public-source research
  • Source links and verification notes
  • Transparent fit scoring and data gaps
  • Questions for the buyer's own validation

Not included

  • Personal emails, personal phones, or social profiles
  • Credit ratings, legal opinions, or compliance guarantees
  • Mass outreach, misrepresented relationships, or purchased lists
  • Guarantees of reply, distribution, orders, or revenue
